Micro Interactions

Every click should feel satisfying.

Buttons compress slightly before expanding.

Cards lift with soft shadows.

Icons animate when hovered.

Input fields glow elegantly on focus.

Success actions trigger a subtle ripple.

Counters animate only when entering the viewport.

Every page transition fades and slides smoothly.

Mobile Experience

The mobile version should not be a simplified copy of the desktop experience—it should feel intentionally designed.

Full-screen immersive hero with stacked animated phone mockups.
Thumb-friendly floating navigation with smooth hide/show behavior while scrolling.
Swipeable feature cards with snap scrolling.
Sticky bottom "Open Account" CTA that gently appears after the hero.
Optimized parallax and motion effects for smooth 60fps performance.
Touch gestures trigger subtle card tilts, ripples, and haptic-style visual feedback.
Lightweight animations to preserve battery life while maintaining a premium feel.


Overall User Journey

The experience should unfold like a story:

A powerful first impression that immediately communicates innovation and trust.
Interactive discovery where users naturally explore features through motion rather than dense text.
Proof and confidence using live dashboards, statistics, customer stories, and security demonstrations.
Emotional connection with beautiful visuals, delightful micro-interactions, and seamless transitions.
A compelling finish with a strong call-to-action that makes opening an account feel like the obvious next step.

The end result should feel less like a conventional banking website and more like a premium fintech experience—combining the visual polish of Apple, the smooth interactions of Linear, the modern feel of Revolut, and the trustworthiness expected from a world-class financial institution.
